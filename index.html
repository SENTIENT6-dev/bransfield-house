About the building

Room to live, in the heart of Etobicoke

Bransfield House was built by Bransfield Construction and registered as York Condominium Corporation No. 86 in August 1972. Its homes are large by today's standards: two- and three-bedroom layouts of roughly 1,270 to 1,550 square feet, twelve to a floor, with balconies.

The building is professionally managed, with a management office on the ground floor, a live-in superintendent, and a security desk in the lobby every evening and overnight.

For buyers and renters

What the monthly fees cover

The building is bulk-metered, so the main utilities are part of the common expenses rather than separate bills.

  • Heat, electricity and water
  • Bell Fibe internet and TV in every suite
  • An assigned indoor parking space for each suite
  • Use of the pool, saunas, gym, library, recreational room and hobby room
  • Building insurance, maintenance, staff and security
  • Contributions to the reserve fund

A status certificate, ordered through the management office, is the authoritative record of a unit's fees and what they include.

Internet and TV included

Bell Fibe in every suite

Under the building's agreement with Bell, each suite gets this package on Bell's fibre network as part of the monthly fees.

Fibe Internet

  • Up to 1.5 Gbps download
  • Up to 940 Mbps upload
  • Unlimited usage
  • Modem, plus a Wi-Fi pod if needed

Fibe TV

  • The "Better" channel package
  • Crave with Starz
  • Time shifting
  • Up to 2 smart (Android) 4K PVRs

Installation is included. Each household activates the service with Bell, and anything added on top, such as extra channels, more receivers or home phone, is billed by Bell to the resident. How to activate

Location

Close to everything in the west end

Bransfield House sits at Burnhamthorpe Road and The West Mall, beside Highway 427, with quick connections to Highway 401, the Gardiner Expressway and Pearson Airport. TTC buses stop nearby and run to Kipling Station for the subway and GO trains.

On the grounds you will find a children's playground, a green space behind the building, a fenced off-leash area for residents' dogs, visitor parking and two electric vehicle charging spots.
