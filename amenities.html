Open daily, 7:00 am to 11:00 pm

Indoor pool

The pool is unsupervised, so you swim at your own risk. Please shower before you get in.

  • Anyone under 15 must be with a resident aged 18 or older, and can use the pool between 9:00 am and 9:00 pm.
  • Children under 2, and anyone in diapers, are not allowed in the pool area.
  • No more than 10 people in the pool and 20 in the pool area at once.
  • Up to two guests per suite, and the resident must be swimming with them.
  • Proper swimwear only. Tie back long hair or wear a cap.
  • No diving or jumping, no inflatables, no glass, and no food or drinks.

Rules, section 16

Open daily, 7:00 am to 11:00 pm

Saunas

The saunas are beside the pool and are also unsupervised.

  • No children under 12. Ages 12 to 16 must be with a resident aged 18 or older.
  • Shower first, wear swimwear or a towel, and sit on a towel.
  • Never put water, oils or any liquid on the heater.
  • Up to two guests per suite, with the resident present.

Rules, section 17

Weekdays 6 am to 10 pm · Weekends and holidays 8 am to 8 pm

Gym

Cardio machines, weight machines and free weights. Use your fob to get in and keep the door closed.

  • No one under 12. Ages 12 to 15 must be with an adult.
  • One guest per resident at a time.
  • Wear athletic shoes and workout clothes. No sandals or bare feet.
  • Wipe down equipment after use and put weights back on the rack. Do not drop or slam them.
  • Headphones for music. Water bottles are fine; no food and no glass.
  • Skipping ropes are not allowed.

Rules, section 15

Open daily, 7:00 am to 11:00 pm

Library

A quiet lounge with books, armchairs and tables for reading or working.

  • Water in a plastic or metal bottle only. No other food or drinks.
  • Children under 14 must be with a resident aged 18 or older.
  • Street clothes, please: no swimwear or robes.
  • No pets.

Rules, section 13

Open daily, 10:00 am to 11:00 pm

Recreational room

A pool table, foosball and arcade games.

  • Write your name and suite number on the register to reserve the table. Sessions are 60 minutes, starting on the hour, one booking at a time.
  • Anyone under 16 must be with a resident aged 16 or older.
  • Up to two guests per resident.
  • No food, drinks or smoking. Put the equipment back when you finish.

By reservation

Party rooms

Two party rooms with kitchens can be reserved by residents for private gatherings.

RoomGuestsRental fee
Party Room AUp to 59$150 per day

  • A $50 cleaning fee is added to each booking. There is no security deposit, but any damage or extra cleaning is charged to the unit.
  • The resident who books must be present for the whole event.
  • Music at a moderate level until 11:00 pm. The room must be empty by 1:00 am.
  • Cancel at least 72 hours ahead or the rental fee still applies.

How to book

Contact the management office to check the date, then return the signed form: Party Room A form or Party Room B form.

Around the building

Hobby room, outdoors, parking and storage

  • Hobby room

    A room for residents' hobbies and projects, on the basement level. Ask the office about access.

  • Playground and green space

    A children's playground and a lawn behind the building. The playground is unsupervised; children under 10 need an adult with them.

  • Dog off-leash area

    A fenced area for dogs that live in the building. Get a key from the office for a $10 refundable deposit, stay with your dog and clean up.

  • EV charging

    Two pay-per-use electric vehicle charging spots, first come, first served, once a day per resident. Move your car within 15 minutes of the charge ending.

  • Bike room and rack

    Indoor bike room spaces are rented by the year ($25) through the office. Bikes on the outdoor rack must be registered with the office.

  • Storage lockers

    Lockers are assigned to owners who live in the building, first come, first served. Ask the office about availability.

  • Visitor parking

    Visitors register their plate at the security desk. Overnight stays need a permit. See parking and visitors.

Fees, hours and rules are set by the Board and can change. The full wording is in the Rules and Regulations (PDF).
