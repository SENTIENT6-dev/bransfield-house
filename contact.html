Emergencies

In every emergency, also call the superintendent, Waqar Khan.

647-534-6258

Call 911 first

When someone's life or safety is at risk

  • Fire or smoke
  • A medical emergency
  • A crime in progress, or anyone in danger
  • A smell of gas

Then call the superintendent.

No 911 needed

Urgent problems with the building

  • Flooding or a water leak
  • No heat, power or water
  • Someone stuck in an elevator who is not hurt or unwell
  • A door, gate or garage door that will not close or open

Call the superintendent right away.

If you cannot reach him, call the management office during the day (416-621-7042), the security desk in the evening and overnight (437-212-9473), or ICON Property Management's after-hours emergency line (437-294-3322).
